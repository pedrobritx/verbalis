# Translation, reimagined.
## Meet Verbalis.
The modern translation IDE built for professionals.
Verbalis is a next-generation computer-assisted translation platform designed for translators, revisers, editors, localization managers, and multilingual teams. It combines the power of professional CAT tools with the flexibility of a modern development environment.
AI is your copilot—not your replacement.
## Built for human translators.
Professional translation is more than replacing words.
It requires context, terminology, consistency, research, revision, collaboration, and hundreds of small decisions that only experienced translators can make.
Verbalis embraces that reality.
Instead of trying to automate translators away, it gives them better tools.
Work faster without sacrificing quality.
Keep complete control over every decision.
Review changes with confidence.
Let AI assist, never dictate.
